These numbered PNGs are the illustrations used by the Introduction book. Replace
the image files to edit the artwork, but keep their filenames and .meta files.
Edit page descriptions in Assets/Resources/Storybooks/Introduction.asset.

For other books, put numbered images in that book's Pages folder and add them to
its StorybookData asset with a description for each page. Right-click goes forward;
left-click goes back, and either button works anywhere in the view.
